# Commercial license

CVfacile is released under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). It is free and fits most uses.

## When do you need a commercial license?

| Use | License needed |
|---|---|
| Building your own CV, including for a job search | None (AGPL, free) |
| A recruiter, school or non-profit generating CVs internally | None (AGPL, free) |
| An AGPL open source project that includes CVfacile | None (AGPL, free) |
| A SaaS, app or paid service that includes CVfacile **without publishing all its code under the AGPL** | **Commercial license** |
| Redistributing a modified CVfacile in a closed-source product | **Commercial license** |

## What the commercial license includes

- The right to use, modify and embed CVfacile without the AGPL obligations.
- Fees agreed case by case: a yearly flat fee or a share of the revenue tied to the use of CVfacile.

To get a commercial license, [open an issue](https://github.com/nem95/CVFacile/issues/new?title=Commercial%20license) titled "Commercial license", or contact [@nem95](https://github.com/nem95) on GitHub.
